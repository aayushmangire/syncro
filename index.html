01 — The Crisis

Adding roads makes
traffic worse.

In 1968, mathematician Dietrich Braess proved that adding a new road to a congested network can increase total travel time for every driver. GPS apps each optimize for one person and ignore the system — creating the Price of Anarchy.

When millions take identical "shortcuts", local bottlenecks trigger city-wide ripple cascades, burning massive amounts of fuel while vehicles sit stationary.

The Braess network — the shortcut A→B makes everyone slower.
